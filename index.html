    How to take part

    Anyone can speak at a council meeting or send the council a comment. Check each meeting's agenda for the Zoom link and any changes.

    In person
    Regular meetings are held in the School District Board Room, 1231 Addison St., Berkeley.
    On Zoom
    Use the Zoom link on the agenda and raise your hand when public comment opens. By phone, dial the number on the agenda and press *9 to ask to speak.
    In writing
    Email council@berkeleyca.gov to send a comment for the public record. Name the agenda item you're writing about.
    Watch
    Meetings are broadcast live with captions on B-TV (Channel 33) and streamed online. Use the Watch link next to each meeting.
