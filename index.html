Upcoming council meetings

Agendas as soon as the City Clerk posts them

    How to take part

    Anyone can speak at a council meeting or send the council a comment. Each meeting's agenda has the Zoom link and any changes.

    In person

    Regular meetings are held in the School District Board Room, 1231 Addison St., Berkeley.

    On Zoom

    Use the Zoom link on the agenda and raise your hand when public comment opens. By phone, dial the number on the agenda and press *9.

    In writing

    Email council@berkeleyca.gov to add a comment to the public record. Name the agenda item you're writing about.

    Watch

    Meetings air live with captions on B-TV (Channel 33) and stream online. Use the Watch link next to each meeting.
